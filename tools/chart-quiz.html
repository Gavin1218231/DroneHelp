Sectional Chart Quiz

Practice identifying airspace classes, special use airspace, obstacles, and airport symbology on simplified sectional chart snippets.

How this quiz works

This is a learning tool that uses simplified illustrations drawn in the browser — they are not actual FAA chart excerpts. The shapes, colors, and labels follow the real sectional chart conventions you will see on the Part 107 exam:

  • Blue lines = Class B (solid), Class D (dashed), or restricted-area hashing
  • Magenta lines = Class C (solid), Class E surface (dashed), or MOA hashing
  • Faded blue or magenta shading = Class E airspace floor (1,200 ft or 700 ft AGL)
  • Blue airport symbol = towered · Magenta airport symbol = non-towered

For each scenario, pick the best answer. You will see the correct answer and a brief explanation immediately. Your final score appears after the last question.
