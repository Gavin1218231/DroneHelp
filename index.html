About the FAA Part 107 Exam

Exam Format

  • 60 multiple-choice questions
  • 3 answer choices per question (A, B, C)
  • 120 minutes to complete
  • 70% passing score (42/60 correct)
  • Taken at FAA-approved testing center

Requirements

  • Must be at least 16 years old
  • Able to read, speak, and understand English
  • Be in physical and mental condition to safely fly
  • Pass TSA security vetting
  • Testing fee: approximately $175

What to Bring

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Testing center confirmation
  • Basic (non-programmable) calculator allowed
  • Supplementary materials provided at center
  • No phones or smart devices in testing room

After Passing

  • Apply for Remote Pilot Certificate via IACRA
  • TSA background check required
  • Temporary certificate issued upon approval
  • Permanent card mailed within weeks
  • Recurrent training required every 24 months
