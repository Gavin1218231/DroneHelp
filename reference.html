Operations Over People Categories

Category Weight Limit Key Requirement
Cat 1 ≤ 0.55 lbs No exposed rotating parts that could lacerate
Cat 2 > 0.55 lbs Must not cause serious injury (FAA-accepted means of compliance)
Cat 3 > 0.55 lbs Cannot sustain flight over open-air assembly; must not cause serious injury
Cat 4 Any Requires airworthiness certificate and operating limitations

Mnemonics & Memory Aids

  • IMSAFE (Preflight self-assessment): Illness, Medication, Stress, Alcohol, Fatigue, Eating
  • PAVE (Risk assessment): Pilot, Aircraft, enVironment, External pressures
  • 3P Model (Decision making): Perceive, Process, Perform
  • 3 H’s (Density altitude): High altitude, Hot temperature, Humid conditions
  • Class G = Go! No ATC authorization needed
  • Thunderstorm stages: Cumulus → Mature → Dissipating
  • Temp + Dew Point converge = Fog/clouds forming

Remote ID Quick Facts

  • Three compliance methods: Standard Remote ID, Broadcast Module, FRIA
  • Must broadcast: drone ID, location, altitude, control station location, timestamp
  • Required since: March 16, 2024
  • FRIAs established by community-based organizations

Pre-Flight Checklist Summary

  1. Check weather conditions (METARs, TAFs, TFRs)
  2. Verify airspace authorization if needed (LAANC/DroneZone)
  3. IMSAFE self-assessment
  4. Inspect airframe, propellers, and landing gear
  5. Check battery charge and condition
  6. Verify GPS signal and compass calibration
  7. Test control link and failsafe/lost link procedures
  8. Confirm Remote ID is broadcasting
  9. Brief visual observer (if using one)
  10. Survey launch/landing area for hazards

Exam Day Tips

  • Bring government-issued photo ID
  • Basic (non-programmable) calculator is allowed
  • Supplemental materials (charts, legends) are provided
  • 60 questions, 120 minutes, 70% (42/60) to pass
  • Process of elimination: rule out obviously wrong answers first
  • Flag tough questions and return to them
  • Watch for “trick” wording: “always,” “never,” “must” vs “should”
  • Focus study time on Operations (35–45%) and Regulations/Airspace (15–25% each)
